Tata-PSMC Semiconductor Fabrication Mega-Facility in Dholera Enters Cleanroom Installation Ahead of Schedule

India's first commercial 300mm silicon wafer plant achieves structural completion in Dholera Special Investment Region, preparing to fabricate 50,000 wafers monthly for AI, automotive, and defense.

DHOLERA, GUJARAT — In a historic leap toward technological self-reliance, the Tata Electronics and Powerchip Semiconductor Manufacturing Corporation (PSMC) semiconductor fabrication plant in Dholera has formally commenced cleanroom mechanical fitting ahead of schedule, setting the stage for India's first commercially fabricated 300mm silicon wafers to roll off the production line in 2026.

Building the Cleanroom Infrastructure for Sub-Micron Precision

The ₹91,000-crore ($11 Billion) mega-foundry spans over 160 acres within the Dholera Special Investment Region (DSIR). Constructing a modern semiconductor wafer fab requires engineering tolerances unprecedented in traditional civil construction: Class 1000 and Class 100 cleanrooms where vibration must be suppressed below 3 micrometers per second and air filtration removes microscopic particulate matter down to 10 nanometers.

The President of Tata Electronics remarked: 'Semiconductor manufacturing is the bedrock of modern digital sovereignty. From artificial intelligence data centers and high-speed electric locomotives to guided missile systems, silicon chips are the new oil. Dholera will anchor an entire domestic ecosystem of suppliers, chemical refiners, and precision packaging houses.'

Process Nodes: 28nm, 40nm, and 90nm for Mission-Critical Applications

The fab will initially process 300mm silicon wafers across mature and legacy nodes—specifically 28nm, 40nm, and 90nm. While sub-3nm nodes dominate consumer smartphones, mature nodes represent over 70% of total global semiconductor volume, powering automotive engine control units (ECUs), industrial IoT controllers, power management ICs, and AI edge accelerators.

Dholera Fab 1 Engineering & Operational Parameters

  • • Total Capital Investment: ₹91,000 Crore ($11 Billion) under India Semiconductor Mission (ISM).
  • • Peak Monthly Capacity: 50,000 Wafer Starts Per Month (WSPM) on 300mm silicon substrates.
  • • Process Technologies: 28nm high-k metal gate (HKMG), 40nm, and 90nm logic and power management.
  • • Ultra-Pure Water & Power: 100 MLD recycling water plant and uninterrupted dual 220 kV power substation.

Catalyzing the Compound Semiconductor and OSAT Ecosystem

Adjacent to the Dholera fab, global chemical purifiers and testing equipment vendors have acquired industrial plots, while Micron's ATMP facility in Sanand and Tata's OSAT facility in Jagiroad, Assam, create an end-to-end domestic silicon assembly and packaging pipeline.

Transforms India from a chip consumer into a sovereign semiconductor manufacturing powerhouse, insulating critical industries from global supply shocks.
