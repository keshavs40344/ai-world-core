Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay Prioritizes State Anthem Over Centre’s Vande Mataram Push

Chief Minister Vijay reiterated that the Tamil state anthem must be given top priority in Tamil Nadu and that his administration will not compromise on linguistic rights. The statement comes as the central government intensifies its promotion of Vande Mataram as a national song. Officials confirmed the stance reflects the state’s long‑standing policy to protect regional language heritage.
