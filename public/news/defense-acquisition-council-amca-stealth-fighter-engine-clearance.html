Defense Acquisition Council Accords ₹65,000 Crore Acceptance of Necessity for Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA)

Cabinet Committee on Security paves path for five prototypes of fifth-generation twin-engine stealth fighter with internal weapons bays and supercruise capability.

NEW DELHI — In what defense planners describe as the single most critical leap in modernizing the Indian Air Force's combat edge, the Defence Acquisition Council (DAC) chaired by the Defence Minister has accorded Acceptance of Necessity (AoN) for the indigenous Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA) Mark-1 and Mark-2 programs, greenlighting ₹65,000 Crore for prototype fabrication, flight testing, and production-line tooling.

Core Investigative Findings & Key Facts

  • ▪Defence Acquisition Council approves ₹65,000 Crore for 5th-generation stealth fighter AMCA.
  • ▪Features serpentine intake ducts, radar-absorbent composites, and internal weapons bay.
  • ▪Mark-2 variant to feature co-developed 110kN turbofan engine with full IP transfer.
  • ▪Private sector aerospace primes Tata and L&T partnered in production SPV.

Fifth-Generation Stealth Geometry and Radar Absorbence

The AMCA design, developed by the Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA) in partnership with the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), incorporates serpentine air intake ducts that completely shield engine turbine blades from hostile radar illumination. Coupled with radar-absorbent materials (RAM) and flush composite external sensor apertures, the airframe achieves a frontal radar cross-section (RCS) comparable to international fifth-generation benchmarks.

Chief of Air Staff Air Chief Marshal addressed senior commanders, emphasizing that the first prototype flight is targeted for late 2028. 'AMCA is not merely an airframe; it is an airborne node in our sovereign integrated multi-domain battle management mesh, equipped with advanced active electronically scanned array (AESA) radar, infrared search-and-track (IRST), and indigenous electronic warfare suites,' he affirmed.

Co-Development of 110kN High-Thrust Jet Engine

While the initial Mark-1 variant will be powered by twin GE-414 engines, the Mark-2 iteration will feature an entirely indigenous 110 kilo-Newton (kN) high-thrust turbofan engine co-developed with an international propulsion prime under 100% intellectual property transfer to India. The engine will enable supercruise—sustained supersonic flight at Mach 1.5 without the fuel penalty of engaging afterburners.

AMCA Technical Specifications

  • • Crew Configuration: Single-Seat Twin-Engine Stealth Multi-Role Fighter
  • • Internal Weapons Bay Payload: 1,500 kg (Astra Mark-3 beyond-visual-range missiles & Smart Precision Bombs)
  • • Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW): 25,000 kg with internal fuel capacity of 6,500 kg
  • • Avionics: Uttam Gallium Nitride (GaN) AESA Radar & Optical Laser Target Designation Pods

Industrial Partnership with Private Aerospace Primes

In a historic departure from monolithic public sector execution, the production phase will be spearheaded by a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) joint venture comprising HAL and Indian private defense primes including Tata Advanced Systems and Larsen & Toubro. Over 400 domestic Tier-2 and Tier-3 micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) will supply precision structural forgings, avionics wiring looms, and software modules.

Strategic & Civic Impact Analysis

Decisively elevates India into the sovereign league of 5th-generation fighter builders, neutralizing regional stealth disparity and reducing foreign arms dependency.

रक्षा अधिग्रहण परिषद (DAC) ने भारतीय वायुसेना के लिए 5वीं पीढ़ी के एडवांस्ड मीडियम कॉम्बैट एयरक्राफ्ट (AMCA) परियोजना को ₹65,000 करोड़ की लागत से मंजूरी दे दी है। यह विमान रडार को चकमा देने वाली स्टील्थ तकनीक और आंतरिक हथियार कक्ष (Internal Weapons Bay) से लैस होगा।

110kN के शक्तिशाली इंजन का निर्माण

विमान के दूसरे संस्करण (Mark-2) के लिए 110 किलोन्यूटन क्षमता का नया जेट इंजन विकसित किया जाएगा, जो विमान को बिना आफ्टरबर्नर ऑन किए सुपरसोनिक गति (Supercruise) से उड़ने में सक्षम बनाएगा। निजी क्षेत्र की टाटा और एलएंडटी जैसी कंपनियां भी इस परियोजना में साझेदार होंगी।
