Israel Orders Evacuation of Unauthorized West Bank Settlements

The Israeli government announced that Prime Minister Netanyahu has ordered the evacuation of several West Bank settlements that were established without official approval. These outposts are deemed illegal under both international law and Israeli statutes, marking a notable shift in settlement policy.
