Maharashtra Ministers Push for DJ-Free Festivals Over Health Risks

State ministers in Maharashtra have urged that festivals be organized without DJs and high‑powered loudspeakers, citing documented health hazards linked to excessive noise. The call has gained traction as activist Vidyanand Bapat of Pune highlights the chronic noise‑pollution problem during celebrations. Officials are reviewing existing event‑permits to align with the proposed restrictions.
