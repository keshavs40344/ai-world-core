RBI Monetary Policy Committee Maintains Repo Rate at 6.50% Citing Robust 7.4% GDP Trajectory

Central bank emphasizes disinflation continuity while forecasting sustained industrial expansion and resilient corporate balance sheets.

MUMBAI — In its bi-monthly monetary policy announcement, the Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) has resolved unanimously to maintain the benchmark policy repo rate unchanged at 6.50%, underscoring the macroeconomic resilience of the Indian economy amid turbulent global interest rate environments.

Core Investigative Findings & Key Facts

  • ▪Repo rate kept unchanged at 6.50% by unanimous vote of the Monetary Policy Committee.
  • ▪Real GDP growth forecast elevated to a robust 7.4% for the fiscal year.
  • ▪India's foreign exchange reserves provide a solid cushion at $684.2 Billion.
  • ▪Banking system credit expansion remains elevated at 15.8% with NPAs down to 2.8%.

Calibrated Stance on Inflation Glidepath

Delivering the monetary policy statement, the RBI Governor reaffirmed the central bank's commitment to ensuring inflation progressively aligns with the target of 4.0% on a durable basis, while continuing to support economic expansion. Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation for the current fiscal is projected at 4.5%, supported by disciplined agricultural supply-chain logistics and stable core non-food manufacturing prices.

The MPC retained its baseline policy stance of 'Withdrawal of Accommodation', highlighting that excess system liquidity must be dynamically managed through fine-tuning variable rate reverse repo (VRRR) auctions to mitigate inflationary second-round spillover risks.

GDP Growth Projections Anchor Global Confidence

India's real GDP growth for FY2026-27 is projected at an authoritative 7.4%, buoyed by double-digit capital goods production, sustained rural consumption revival, and historic infrastructure capex execution by both Central and State governments. Private sector gross fixed capital formation (GFCF) expanded at 8.2%, marking the fastest private investment cycle in over seven years.

Macroeconomic Projections Summary

  • • Benchmark Policy Repo Rate: 6.50% (Unchanged)
  • • Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) Rate: 6.25% | Marginal Standing Facility (MSF): 6.75%
  • • Real GDP Growth Forecast: 7.4% for FY 2026-27
  • • Headline CPI Inflation Target: 4.5% anchored within tolerance band
  • • Foreign Exchange Reserves: Healthy buffer standing at $684.2 Billion

Market & Banking Reaction

Indian equity benchmarks BSE Sensex and Nifty 50 traded with firm positive momentum, rising 0.6% following the policy broadcast. Banking sector leaders noted that credit growth remains robust at 15.8% year-on-year, accompanied by historic gross non-performing asset (GNPA) lows of 2.8% across scheduled commercial banks.
