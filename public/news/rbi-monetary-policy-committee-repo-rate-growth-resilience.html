RBI Monetary Policy Committee Maintains Repo Rate at 6.50% Citing Robust 7.4% GDP Trajectory

Central bank emphasizes disinflation continuity while forecasting sustained industrial expansion and resilient corporate balance sheets.

MUMBAI — Following a detailed three-day macroeconomic review, the Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) has unanimously resolved to maintain the benchmark policy repo rate at 6.50%, underscoring the resilience of domestic manufacturing, capital expenditure surges, and anchored consumer inflation.

Macroeconomic Growth Engine Resilient

Presenting the bi-monthly statement, the RBI Governor highlighted that real GDP growth for the current fiscal is projected at a solid 7.4%, buoyed by strong rural consumption, record infrastructure disbursements, and robust bank credit expansion exceeding 14.8% year-on-year. Core inflation has moderated comfortably within the central bank's medium-term tolerance band of 4.0% ± 2%.

The Governor noted: 'The domestic financial system stands on an unshakeable footing. Gross Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) across scheduled commercial banks have plummeted to a decade-low of 2.8%, while capital adequacy ratios exceed 16.5%. Our policy stance remains anchored on the withdrawal of accommodation to ensure inflation aligns durably to the 4% target while fostering sustainable expansion.'

Liquidity Management and Currency Stability

The MPC underscored that foreign exchange reserves stand near historic peaks of over $685 billion, providing an extensive buffer against global interest rate differentials and speculative currency pressures. Systemic liquidity will continue to be calibrated via Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auctions to maintain overnight interbank rates aligned with the policy repo rate.

Key Financial Projections & Indicators

  • • Projected Real GDP Growth: 7.4% for Fiscal Year 2026-27
  • • Headline CPI Inflation Target: 4.2% for the ongoing quarter
  • • Scheduled Commercial Bank Gross NPA Ratio: 2.8% (12-year low)
  • • Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) Rate: 6.25% • Marginal Standing Facility (MSF): 6.75%

Market and Industry Reaction

Equities rallied following the announcement, with the benchmark BSE Sensex gaining over 480 points and the Nifty 50 crossing key resistance zones. Banking, automotive, and infrastructure counters led the advance, driven by institutional confidence in sustained credit demand without near-term monetary tightening risks.
