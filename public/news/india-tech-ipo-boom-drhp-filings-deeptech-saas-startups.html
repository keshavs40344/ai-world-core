Profitable Unit Economics Define the New IPO Class

Unlike the speculative consumer internet listings of 2021 that relied on heavy cash-burn, over 80% of the new IPO applicants have recorded positive EBITDA for at least eight consecutive quarters. Leading the charge are enterprise cloud automation platforms, AI vision inspection software providers, and indigenous satellite constellation operators boasting gross margins exceeding 70%.

A Senior Managing Director at Kotak Investment Banking noted: 'The Indian public market has evolved. Domestic institutional mutual funds and high-net-worth investors are rewarding companies with durable free cash flow and global enterprise contracts. Indian tech founders are no longer compelled to list on Nasdaq to find deep liquidity; Mumbai is providing superior valuations.'

Reverse-Flipping Wave to Sovereign Indian Jurisdiction

Significantly, nine of the 14 filing companies previously relocated their corporate domiciles back to India from Singapore and Delaware—a process colloquially termed 'reverse flipping'. Tax regulatory simplifications enacted in recent union budgets and streamlined merger approval timelines have catalyzed this homecoming of domestic intellectual property.

Upcoming Tech IPO Cohort Metrics

  • • Cumulative Capital Target: $12.4 Billion (₹1,04,000 Crore) across primary and secondary offers.
  • • Cohort Profitability: 82% of filing entities demonstrate positive trailing 12-month EBITDA.
  • • Average Overseas Revenue Share: 62% of revenue generated from North American and European clients.
  • • Anchor Investor Commitments: Domestic mutual funds guarantee 45% of institutional book-building.

Wealth Democratization for Engineers & Early Employees

The listings are expected to unlock over ₹35,000 crore in employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) liquidity across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune, fueling a virtuous flywheel of angel investment into the next generation of early-stage deep-tech startups.
