Delhi Building Collapse Claims Five Lives, Eleven Rescued; FIR Filed Against Owner

A five‑storey building in Delhi collapsed, killing at least five people and rescuing eleven from the rubble. Police are filing an FIR against the building's owner, Anand Bansal of Hostel Daze, as investigations reveal ongoing basement repairs and reports of a weak foundation with water seepage.
