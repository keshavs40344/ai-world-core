PUNE — In a giant leap for sovereign artificial intelligence infrastructure, the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) under the National Supercomputing Mission has officially commissioned AIRAWAT-2, a 100-petaflop AI supercomputing cluster housed at the C-DAC campus in Pune.

Architectural Blueprint and Sovereign Compute

AIRAWAT-2 represents India's most powerful AI compute facility, constructed using indigenous 'Rudra' dual-socket blade servers interconnected via C-DAC's proprietary Trinetra high-bandwidth, low-latency fabric. Equipped with advanced liquid immersion cooling technology, the supercomputer achieves an exceptional Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) rating of 1.18, making it among the most energy-efficient computing installations in Asia.

The Director General of C-DAC announced: 'Without sovereign computational infrastructure, artificial intelligence development becomes captive to foreign cloud monopolies. AIRAWAT-2 guarantees that our researchers, universities, and deep-tech startups can train trillion-parameter multilingual foundational models entirely within Indian territory.'

Catalyzing Healthcare, Genomics, and Materials Science

The cluster is immediately designated to support mission-critical national priorities, including molecular docking simulations for indigenous drug discovery, high-resolution monsoon meteorological modeling, and the training of sovereign Indic language LLMs covering all 22 scheduled national languages.

AIRAWAT-2 Performance Specifications

  • • Peak Computational Throughput: 100 Petaflops (FP16 AI precision).
  • • Server Architecture: Indigenous 'Rudra' multi-core servers with Trinetra interconnect.
  • • Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE): 1.18 achieved via direct-to-chip liquid cooling.
  • • High-Speed Storage Tier: 50 Petabytes of NVMe tiered parallel filesystem.

Democratic Cloud Access for Startups and Academia

Under the IndiaAI Mission allocation framework, 70% of AIRAWAT-2's compute capacity will be made accessible to accredited Indian academic institutions, researchers, and early-stage AI startups through subsidized government compute vouchers, preventing capital constraints from stifling domestic innovation.

⚡ Strategic & Civil Ramifications

Provides technological sovereignty in foundational artificial intelligence, ensuring Indian researchers develop advanced generative models without foreign compute dependence.
