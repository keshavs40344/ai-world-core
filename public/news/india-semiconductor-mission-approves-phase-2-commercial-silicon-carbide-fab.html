India Semiconductor Mission Clears ₹38,000 Crore Phase-2 Commercial Silicon Carbide (SiC) Power Fab

Compound semiconductor facility to produce next-generation power chips for electric vehicles, solar inverters, and high-speed rail networks.

NEW DELHI — In a decisive acceleration of India's indigenous electronic hardware self-reliance, the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister has approved Phase-2 of the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM), greenlighting a ₹38,000 Crore commercial Silicon Carbide (SiC) and Gallium Nitride (GaN) power semiconductor fabrication facility in Dholera, Gujarat.

Core Investigative Findings & Key Facts

  • ▪Union Cabinet clears ₹38,000 Crore commercial Silicon Carbide (SiC) power fab in Dholera.
  • ▪Produces high-voltage 1200V/1700V MOSFETs for electric vehicles, renewables, and rail.
  • ▪Boasts 45,000 eight-inch wafer starts per month capacity with Taiwanese partnership.
  • ▪Creates 4,800 direct advanced cleanroom engineering positions.

High-Efficiency Compound Semiconductor Leadership

While traditional silicon chips dominate standard computing microprocessors, compound semiconductor materials like Silicon Carbide (SiC) operate at exponentially higher voltages, switching frequencies, and thermal tolerances. These specialized chips are the foundational electronic organs of modern electric vehicle (EV) drivetrains, fast-charging stations, utility solar inverters, and Vande Bharat high-speed railway traction motors.

The approved facility, developed in joint venture with leading Taiwanese and European fabrication partners, boasts a capacity of 45,000 eight-inch (200mm) wafer starts per month (WSPM). Production is slated to formally commence in early 2027.

Complete Domestic Ecosystem Buildout

Unlike isolated assembly plants, the Phase-2 approval integrates raw crystal boule growing, precision slicing, epitaxial layer deposition, front-end photolithography, and advanced automated testing and packaging (OSAT/ATMP). Over 30 ancillary supply chain units producing ultra-pure specialty chemicals, quartz carriers, and electronic-grade gases have already secured adjacent industrial land allocations.

Project Specifications & Metrics

  • • Total Capital Outlay: ₹38,400 Crore (Under 50% fiscal parity support from ISM)
  • • Location: Dholera Special Investment Region (SIR), Gujarat
  • • Technology Nodes: 1200V & 1700V Silicon Carbide MOSFETs and Schottky Diodes
  • • Direct High-Tech Employment: 4,800 specialized chip design & fab engineering jobs

Securing Automotive & Strategic Defense Supplies

Global automotive OEMs have faced periodic crippling shortages of power semiconductors over the past half-decade. With this facility, Indian automobile manufacturers, aerospace primes, and renewable inverter producers will satisfy over 70% of their critical power electronic requirements domestically, shielding the economy from geopolitical maritime chokepoint disruptions.

Strategic & Civic Impact Analysis

Insulates India's automotive and renewable industries from overseas semiconductor supply chain vulnerabilities while establishing deep domestic hardware manufacturing equity.
