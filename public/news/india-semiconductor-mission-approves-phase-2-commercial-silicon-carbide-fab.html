India Semiconductor Mission Approves Phase-2 Commercial Silicon Carbide Fab and Advanced 3D Packaging Cluster

Union Cabinet clears ₹45,000 crore incentive envelope for power semiconductor fabs, compound semiconductor foundries, and chiplet assembly.

NEW DELHI — In an aggressive expansion of India's sovereign high-technology manufacturing footprint, the Union Cabinet has approved Phase-2 of the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM), greenlighting a ₹45,000 crore capital expenditure program for commercial Silicon Carbide (SiC) and Gallium Nitride (GaN) foundries alongside advanced 3D chip packaging facilities.

Powering the Electric Vehicle and Grid Transformation

While silicon microprocessors dominate compute workloads, wide-bandgap compound semiconductors like Silicon Carbide and Gallium Nitride are indispensable for high-voltage power electronics. Electric vehicle drivetrains, fast-charging stations, solar inverters, and high-speed rail traction motors require SiC chips to operate at high temperatures with minimal resistive energy losses.

The Minister of Electronics and IT stated: 'The first phase of the semiconductor mission established our foundational wafer fabs and OSAT facilities in Dholera and Sanand. Phase-2 directly tackles the high-margin, mission-critical compound semiconductors that power industrial electrification and defense radars. India will no longer be hostage to global supply chain chokepoints.'

Advanced 3D Packaging and Chiplet Ecosystem

Recognizing the approaching physical limits of traditional 2D silicon scaling, Phase-2 places special emphasis on advanced substrate packaging, wafer-level chip-scale packaging (WLCSP), and high-density chiplet interconnects. The approved cluster in Jewar, Uttar Pradesh, will feature cleanroom facilities capable of assembling heterogeneous compute dies with sub-10-micron interconnect pitches.

Phase-2 ISM Capital Allocations

  • • Silicon Carbide (SiC) Commercial Fab: 50,000 wafer starts per month (WSPM) 150mm/200mm facility
  • • Gallium Nitride (GaN) RF Foundry: Dedicated sovereign defense and 5G/6G telecom power amplifiers
  • • Advanced 3D Packaging / OSAT: High-density chiplet assembly line with automated optical inspection
  • • Skilled Workforce Initiative: 25,000 specialized semiconductor design engineers trained via domestic IITs and NITs

Integration with Domestic Fabless Design Startups

The program directly pairs newly authorized manufacturing fabs with India's burgeoning fabless design ecosystem. Over 30 domestic fabless chip design startups registered under the Design-Linked Incentive (DLI) scheme will receive guaranteed prototype shuttle runs at the newly sanctioned foundries, accelerating time-to-market for indigenous microcontrollers and AI accelerators.

⚡ Strategic & Civil Ramifications

Insulates India's automotive, telecom, and defense sectors from foreign chip embargoes while creating a multi-billion dollar export capability.
