Renowned Carnatic Musician S.R. Janakiraman Dies at 98

Veteran Carnatic vocalist and scholar S.R. Janakiraman, aged 98, passed away in Chennai. He was celebrated for his erudite lectures and practical demonstrations that shaped the December music festival season for decades. His death marks the loss of a pivotal figure in the transmission of Carnatic tradition.
