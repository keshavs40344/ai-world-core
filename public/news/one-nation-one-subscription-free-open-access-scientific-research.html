Centrally negotiated ₹6,000 crore consortium grants 18 million college students and faculty unconditional digital access to 13,000 high-impact international journals.

NEW DELHI — In a historic victory for open science and academic equity, the Union Cabinet has approved the groundbreaking 'One Nation, One Subscription' (ONOS) initiative, centrally acquiring nationwide digital access to over 13,000 high-impact international scientific journals for all state and central higher education institutions.

Dismantling Exploitative Publisher Paywalls

Historically, commercial academic publishing oligopolies charged astronomical subscription fees—often exceeding ₹10 crore per year for a single university—forcing cash-strapped state colleges and rural researchers to rely on pirated portals or abandon research entirely. Under ONOS, the central government negotiated a single unified national consortium license covering prestigious publishers including Elsevier, Springer Nature, Wiley, and IEEE.

The Union Minister of Information and Broadcasting noted: 'Knowledge should never be a luxury accessible only to elite institutions. Under One Nation, One Subscription, a faculty member or student at a district college in Assam or Kerala will enjoy the exact same instant digital access to cutting-edge medical and physics research as a scholar at Harvard or Cambridge.'

Unlocking 18 Million Scholars Across 6,000 Institutions

The centralized subscription connects over 6,000 accredited universities, medical colleges, engineering academies, and national research laboratories to the INFLIBNET digital portal. Access is authenticated via the national Academic Bank of Credits ID, allowing scholars to read and download research papers from anywhere in the country.

ONOS National License Highlights

  • • Journal Coverage: Over 13,000 high-impact peer-reviewed scientific journals included.
  • • Beneficiary Community: 18 Million undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD scholars and faculty.
  • • Participating Publishers: Elsevier, Springer Nature, Taylor & Francis, Wiley, ACS, and IEEE.
  • • National Fiscal Savings: Consolidated bargaining reduced aggregate institutional subscription costs by 48%.

Mandatory Open Access for Indian Public Research

In tandem with ONOS, the government enacted a reciprocal mandate requiring all scientific research funded by Indian public tax revenues to be uploaded to open-access national repositories within 12 months of publication, democratizing knowledge globally.

⚡ Strategic & Civil Ramifications

Eliminates academic inequality across Indian higher education and supercharges scientific discovery across regional and state universities.
