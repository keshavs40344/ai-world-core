India and China Conduct First Corps‑Commander Dialogue in Arunachal Pradesh

India and China held their inaugural meeting of senior corps commanders in Arunachal Pradesh, a few days before the BRICS summit where President Xi Jinping is slated to attend. The talks, convened amid reports of heightened activity in the Taksing sector, marked an expansion of the existing military dialogue framework along the disputed border.
