National Education Policy 2020: Autonomous Degree Colleges Transition to Multidisciplinary Research Universities with Unified Credit Bank

UGC notifies statutory guidelines requiring 1,200 autonomous colleges to integrate vocational apprenticeships, humanities, and AI computing into flexible 4-year undergraduate curricula.

NEW DELHI — In an overarching operationalization of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, the University Grants Commission (UGC) has notified new regulatory norms mandating the structural transformation of single-stream higher educational institutions and affiliated degree colleges into large, multidisciplinary degree-granting autonomous universities by 2030.

Dismantling Rigid Disciplinary Silos in Higher Education

Under the freshly notified framework, undergraduate degree programs across over 1,200 recognized colleges will transition fully to the National Higher Education Qualifications Framework (NHEQF). The system dissolves legacy artificial barriers between arts, humanities, commerce, and STEM sciences, allowing engineering students to pursue minors in computational linguistics or public policy, and literature scholars to earn accredited credentials in machine learning and data analysis.

The Chairman of the University Grants Commission emphasized during a national vice-chancellors' conclave: 'The 21st-century knowledge economy does not function in isolated departmental silos. Climate change, quantum computing, and bioethics require minds trained at the intersection of diverse disciplines. Our four-year undergraduate program with multiple entry and exit pathways provides students complete autonomy over their academic trajectories.'

The Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) and DigiLocker Integration

Central to this pedagogical evolution is the nationwide deployment of the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) hosted securely on the sovereign DigiLocker infrastructure. Over 35 million students have already opened verified ABC accounts, enabling seamless credit transfer between central universities, state colleges, and accredited online MOOCs under the SWAYAM portal.

NEP 2020 Higher Education Implementation Metrics

  • • Registered ABC Student Accounts: 35.8 Million digitally verified profiles.
  • • Participating Higher Education Institutions: 2,140 Central, State, and Deemed Universities.
  • • Multiple Entry/Exit Options: Certificate at Year 1, Diploma at Year 2, Degree at Year 3, Honours with Research at Year 4.
  • • Mandatory Internship Component: Minimum 8 academic credits earned through industry or laboratory apprenticeships.

Integrating Experiential Learning and Vocational Apprenticeships

To directly bridge the industry-academia employability mismatch, the UGC regulations mandate that every undergraduate student must complete at least one verified industrial or community apprenticeship. Over 450 corporate entities and national research laboratories (CSIR, ICMR, DRDO) have integrated directly into the national apprenticeship portal, guaranteeing structured stipends and hands-on laboratory experience.
