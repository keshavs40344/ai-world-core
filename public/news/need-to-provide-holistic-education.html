NEW DELHI — In an overarching pedagogical revolution that dismantles century-old colonial educational silos, the University Grants Commission (UGC) alongside the Ministry of Education has formally notified comprehensive statutory guidelines governing the conversion of over 1,200 single-discipline affiliated degree colleges into large, autonomous multidisciplinary degree-granting universities by 2030. Anchored in the National Higher Education Qualifications Framework (NHEQF) and integrated with the sovereign Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) on DigiLocker, the reforms dissolve rigid departmental boundaries, empowering engineering scholars to minor in public policy or computational linguistics, and enabling humanities students to earn accredited credentials in machine learning and data science.

1. Dismantling Departmental Silos: The 2030 Multidisciplinary Mandate

For over a century, higher education in India was organized around rigid, hyper-specialized colleges: standalone engineering institutes, isolated medical colleges, and segregated arts-and-commerce colleges. This compartmentalization produced graduates with narrow vocational skills who struggled to solve complex, multidimensional modern challenges such as climate change, bioethics, artificial intelligence governance, and sustainable urban infrastructure.

Under the newly gazetted UGC regulations, every affiliated undergraduate institution with over 3,000 enrolled students must establish a minimum of four distinct academic faculties: Sciences, Social Sciences & Humanities, Commerce & Management, and Vocational Applied Technologies. Single-stream engineering or commerce colleges that fail to diversify their curricular offerings by 2030 will face automatic phased revocation of their degree-granting affiliation.

The Chairman of the UGC remarked at the National Vice-Chancellors' Conclave: 'The 21st-century knowledge economy does not operate in water-tight compartments. When an engineer builds an autonomous vehicle, they need ethical philosophy; when an economist models fiscal transfers, they need Python data pipelines. The four-year undergraduate program restores holistic intellectual inquiry to Indian higher learning.'

2. The Four-Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUP) with Flexible Exits

Under the reformed FYUP architecture, students are liberated from the punitive 'all-or-nothing' dropout system that previously destroyed the career prospects of millions of students forced to leave college due to economic or family emergencies:

  • • Exit at Year 1: Awarded an Undergraduate Certificate in the chosen field upon completing 40 credits, alongside a mandatory 4-credit vocational internship.
  • • Exit at Year 2: Awarded an Undergraduate Diploma upon completing 80 credits.
  • • Exit at Year 3: Awarded a standard Bachelor's Degree upon completing 120 credits.
  • • Exit at Year 4: Awarded a Bachelor's Degree (Honours with Research) upon completing 160 credits, qualifying the graduate for direct admission into doctoral Ph.D. programs without needing a master's degree.

3. The Academic Bank of Credits: Digital Mobility Across Institutions

At the center of this flexibility is the sovereign Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) hosted on DigiLocker. When a student earns credits at IIT Madras, an online SWAYAM course from IIM Bangalore, and an internship at a CSIR laboratory, every verified credit is cryptographically deposited into their unique ABC digital ledger. Students can pause their studies, work for two years, and re-enroll in another university anywhere in India with zero loss of academic standing.

Pedagogical Innovation and Competency-Based Assessment

The academic transformation shifts the fundamental paradigm of Indian learning from rote memorization to rigorous, project-based inquiry. Under revised curricular mandates, continuous evaluation frameworks replace high-stakes single-examination grading. Students demonstrate mastery through peer-reviewed laboratory research, software code repositories, community public-health audits, and multidisciplinary capstone dissertations evaluated by external industry panels.

The Union Education Secretary emphasized during the National Curriculum Review: 'The objective of higher learning in the 21st century is not to produce compliant test-takers, but autonomous, critical thinkers capable of navigating uncertainty. By merging foundational humanities with applied deep technologies, we are producing graduates who lead global innovation.'

Digital Knowledge Infrastructure and DigiLocker Integration

Seamless nationwide academic mobility is anchored in the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) operating over sovereign DigiLocker APIs. Course credentials, vocational micro-certifications, and research publication records are cryptographically verified and permanently portable. This empowers students to pursue distributed education across premier universities, accredited online platforms, and industry-sponsored apprenticeships with zero administrative friction.

Socio-Economic Equity and Regional Accessibility

Crucially, the reforms dismantle historic urban-rural educational disparities. Through AI-assisted vernacular translation on the Bhashini platform, cutting-edge textbooks, lecture transcripts, and laboratory simulations are accessible across 12 scheduled languages, guaranteeing that talent from non-metropolitan backgrounds enjoys identical academic opportunities as urban peers.

विश्वविद्यालय अनुदान आयोग (UGC) और शिक्षा मंत्रालय ने देश की उच्च शिक्षा प्रणाली को 21वीं सदी के अनुरूप बनाने के लिए ऐतिहासिक दिशा-निर्देश जारी किए हैं। इसके तहत देश के 1,200 से अधिक स्वायत्त कॉलेजों को 2030 तक पूर्ण बहुविषयक (Multidisciplinary) विश्वविद्यालयों में तब्दील किया जाएगा। अंग्रेजों के जमाने से चली आ रही अलग-अलग विषयों (साइंस, आर्ट्स, कॉमर्स) की सख्त दीवारों को पूरी तरह गिरा दिया गया है।

इंजीनियरिंग छात्र पढ़ सकेंगे दर्शनशास्त्र, आर्ट्स के छात्र सीखेंगे एआई

अब किसी भी कॉलेज में केवल एक विषय की पढ़ाई नहीं होगी। इंजीनियरिंग कॉलेज में मानविकी (Humanities), साहित्य और संगीत के विभाग भी होंगे। छात्र अपनी पसंद से मुख्य विषय (Major) और गौण विषय (Minor) चुन सकेंगे। उदाहरण के लिए, कंप्यूटर साइंस का छात्र साइबर सुरक्षा के साथ दर्शनशास्त्र या लोकनीति पढ़ सकता है, और इतिहास का छात्र डेटा साइंस और मशीन लर्निंग के क्रेडिट ले सकता है।

4-वर्षीय ग्रेजुएशन और हर साल एग्जिट की आजादी

नई व्यवस्था में किसी छात्र का साल बर्बाद नहीं होगा। यदि कोई छात्र आर्थिक या पारिवारिक कारणों से पढ़ाई बीच में छोड़ता है, तो उसे नुकसान नहीं होगा: - **1 साल बाद छोड़ने पर:** अंडरग्रेजुएट सर्टिफिकेट मिलेगा। - **2 साल बाद छोड़ने पर:** अंडरग्रेजुएट डिप्लोमा मिलेगा। - **3 साल बाद छोड़ने पर:** बैचलर डिग्री मिलेगी। - **4 साल पूरे करने पर:** बैचलर (ऑनर्स विद रिसर्च) डिग्री मिलेगी, जिससे वह बिना मास्टर डिग्री के सीधे पीएचडी (Ph.D.) में दाखिला ले सकेगा।

डिजीलॉकर पर बने 'एकेडमिक बैंक ऑफ क्रेडिट्स' (ABC) से अब तक 3.5 करोड़ से ज्यादा छात्र जुड़ चुके हैं। इसमें छात्र एक कॉलेज के क्रेडिट दूसरे कॉलेज या ऑनलाइन स्वयं पोर्टल में आसानी से ट्रांसफर कर सकते हैं। साथ ही, डिग्री पाने के लिए न्यूनतम 8 क्रेडिट की इंडस्ट्रियल इंटर्नशिप अनिवार्य कर दी गई है।
