Four Kalady Police Officers Suspended Over Custodial Torture Allegations

Four police officers in Kalady have been suspended after a taxi driver filed a complaint alleging they brutally assaulted him when he was taken into custody on September 2 in connection with a case involving a friend. The department has launched an inquiry to verify the claims and determine further disciplinary action.
