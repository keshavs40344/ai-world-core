NPCI International Expands Real-Time UPI Cross-Border Linkages Across UAE, Singapore and Eurozone Partners

Direct inter-bank settlement reduces overseas remittance transfer costs from 6.2% to under 0.8%, accelerating bilateral commerce.

MUMBAI — In a transformative leap for cross-border financial rails, NPCI International Payments Limited (NIPL), the international arm of the National Payments Corporation of India, has completed multi-corridor real-time linkage connecting the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) directly with the domestic fast-payment networks of the UAE (Aani), Singapore (PayNow), and premier European financial switches.

Democratizing Global Remittances

The bilateral integration enables over 35 million members of the Indian diaspora and international travelers to execute instantaneous, peer-to-peer (P2P) and merchant (P2M) transactions directly using their existing bank apps. By cutting out multi-hop SWIFT correspondent banking intermediaries, the average fee on cross-border transactions has dropped from the global average of 6.2% to less than 0.8%.

Addressing delegates at the Global Fintech Conclave in Mumbai, the CEO of NPCI International stated: 'UPI is no longer just an Indian success story; it is rapidly becoming the world's most scalable public digital infrastructure for frictionless commerce. Today's linkage demonstrates that sovereign real-time payment networks can interoperate without rent-seeking legacy intermediaries.'

Direct Local Currency Settlement Architecture

Crucially, the settlement architecture utilizes direct local currency pairings (INR-AED and INR-SGD), eliminating mandatory conversion through the US Dollar. This direct bilateral settlement shields small businesses and workers from volatile foreign exchange spreads while strengthening sovereign currency reserves.

Cross-Border UPI Integration Metrics

  • • Average Remittance Transfer Time: Reduced from 48 hours to under 4.2 seconds.
  • • Average Transaction Cost: Slashed by 87% compared to traditional legacy wire rails.
  • • Target Bilateral Volume: Projected $18 Billion annualized cross-border flow by 2027.
  • • Participating Banks: 42 Indian commercial banks and 18 international partner institutions.

Eurozone Expansion and Tourism Integration

Following successful merchant trials at iconic French landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, the system is now expanding to broader European retail corridors, allowing Indian tourists and business travelers to scan standard QR codes directly with their domestic mobile banking applications.

⚡ Strategic & Civil Ramifications

Saves billions of dollars in remittance fees for working families while positioning India's digital public infrastructure as the global standard for cross-border settlements.
