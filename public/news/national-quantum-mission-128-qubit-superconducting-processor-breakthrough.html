National Quantum Mission Achieves Milestone with India's First 128-Qubit Superconducting Quantum Processor

Fabricated at Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), the chip maintains coherence times exceeding 120 microseconds.

MUMBAI — In a historic milestone for high-precision quantum information science, physicists and nanofabrication engineers at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), operating under the National Quantum Mission, have announced the successful fabrication and cryogenic testing of India's first 128-qubit superconducting quantum processor.

  • ▪To be integrated into the National Knowledge Network for academic and defense cloud access.

Nanofabrication and Coherence Breakthrough

Fabricated at TIFR's cleanroom facilities in Colaba, the processor utilizes Josephson junction superconducting transmon circuits operating at a cryogenic temperature of 15 millikelvin—colder than deep interstellar space. The system demonstrated average two-qubit gate fidelities of 99.4% and quantum coherence times exceeding 120 microseconds, metrics on par with leading international research laboratories.

The Principal Scientific Adviser to the Government of India remarked: 'Quantum computing is the defining frontier of 21st-century technological superiority. Achieving 128 qubits of coherent superconducting circuits proves that our sovereign materials engineering and microfabrication ecosystem has reached world-class maturity.'

Applications in Cryptography and Material Synthesis

The 128-qubit architecture is specifically engineered to execute fault-tolerant quantum algorithms for post-quantum cryptographic validation, room-temperature superconductor molecular modeling, and real-time algorithmic portfolio optimization for capital markets.

Quantum Processor Benchmark Data

  • • Physical Qubit Count: 128 Transmon Superconducting Qubits.
  • • Operating Temperature: 15 Millikelvin inside helium dilution cryostat.
  • • Average 2-Qubit Gate Fidelity: 99.4% benchmarked via randomized benchmarking.
  • • Quantum Volume: Validated at 2^14 under standard computational benchmarking.

Integration into Sovereign Quantum Cloud Network

The processor will be interfaced with the National Knowledge Network (NKN), providing secure cloud access to university researchers, defense laboratories, and aerospace contractors developing next-generation secure communications.

⚡ Strategic & Civil Ramifications

Elevates India into the top tier of quantum computing nations, safeguarding national cybersecurity against future decryption threats.
