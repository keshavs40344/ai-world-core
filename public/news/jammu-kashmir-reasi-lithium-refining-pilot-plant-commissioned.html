Geological Survey of India & Ministry of Mines Commission First Lithium Refining Pilot Facility in Reasi, J&K

Indigenous hydrometallurgical processing plant achieves 99.5% battery-grade lithium carbonate purity, securing EV supply chains.

JAMMU — In an indispensable breakthrough for national energy transition sovereignty, the Ministry of Mines, in collaboration with the Geological Survey of India (GSI) and CSIR laboratories, has formally commissioned the first commercial-scale pilot lithium refining facility in the Salal-Haimana region of Reasi district, Jammu & Kashmir.

High-Purity Hydrometallurgical Refining

The pilot plant utilizes an eco-friendly hydrometallurgical leaching flow-sheet developed indigenously by the National Metallurgical Laboratory (CSIR-NML). Initial trial runs on clay-hosted bauxitic ore extracted from the 5.9-million-tonne Reasi reserve yielded battery-grade lithium carbonate (Li2CO3) with a certified chemical purity of 99.58%.

The Secretary, Ministry of Mines, declared: 'Import reliance for lithium has been the single greatest bottleneck in India's electric mobility ambitions. Successfully converting our domestic ore into battery-grade lithium carbonate on sovereign soil transforms our strategic positioning and establishes India as an integrated clean energy powerhouse.'

Environmental Safeguards and Closed-Loop Water Recovery

Addressing environmental sensitivities in the fragile Himalayan foothills, the processing complex operates on a closed-loop system that recycles 94% of process water and neutralizes residual tailings before dry-stacking. The facility produces zero acidic wastewater runoff into local river catchments.

Reasi Lithium Pilot Specifications

  • • Certified Output Purity: 99.58% Battery-Grade Lithium Carbonate.
  • • Processing Methodology: Closed-circuit acid leaching with selective precipitation.
  • • Water Recycling Efficiency: 94% closed-loop zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD).
  • • Estimated Total Ore Deposit: 5.9 Million Tonnes inferred resource.

Accelerating Domestic EV Battery Cell Gigafactories

The commissioning of the refining pilot coincides with the operationalization of multiple Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC) battery Gigafactories under the government's Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme, ensuring domestic gigafactories source verified indigenous raw materials by 2027.
