India-France Strategic Defense & Aerospace Partnership: Horizon 2047 Roadmap Accelerates Rafale-M Procurement, Scorpene Submarines, and Joint Jet Engine Tech Transfer

Inter-governmental procurement pact secures 26 maritime strike Rafale fighters for INS Vikrant, three additional air-independent propulsion submarines, and Safran 110kN engine co-development.

NEW DELHI & PARIS — Under the ambitious 'Horizon 2047' bilateral defense and technology treaty, India and France have concluded definitive government-to-government agreements for the procurement of 26 Rafale-Marine carrier-borne fighter aircraft for the Indian Navy, the licensed domestic construction of three additional Scorpene-class attack submarines at Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders, and full 100% intellectual property transfer for a next-generation 110-kilonewton fighter jet engine.

Naval Strike Supremacy with Carrier-Borne Rafale-M

The acquisition of 26 Dassault Rafale-Marine aircraft provides the Indian Navy with an immediate, lethal carrier-borne strike capability for its indigenous aircraft carrier, INS Vikrant. Operating from a ski-jump short take-off but arrested recovery (STOBAR) deck, the Rafale-M integrates the MBDA Meteor beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile and SCALP deep-strike cruise missiles, establishing unmatched maritime domain superiority across the Indian Ocean Region (IOR).

The Chief of the Naval Staff stated: 'The induction of the Rafale-Marine completes a crucial vector of our maritime strike triad. Commonality of maintenance, radar architecture, and weapons inventory with the Indian Air Force's existing fleet of 36 Rafales delivers immense logistical and operational synergies.'

Indigenous Submarine Expansion at Mazagon Dock

Building upon the successful induction of six Kalvari-class (Scorpene) submarines under Project-75, the supplementary contract authorizes Mumbai's Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited (MDL) to construct three additional vessels equipped with indigenously designed Fuel-Cell Air-Independent Propulsion (AIP) plugs developed by the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), granting extended submerged endurance without surfacing.

Bilateral Defense Procurement & Co-Development Parameters

  • • Rafale-Marine Fleet: 26 Aircraft (22 single-seat fighters, 4 twin-seat trainers).
  • • Undersea Fleet Expansion: 3 Scorpene submarines with DRDO Air-Independent Propulsion (AIP).
  • • Joint Engine Co-Development: Safran and Gas Turbine Research Establishment (GTRE) 110 kN turbofan engine for AMCA Mk2.
  • • Technology Transfer: 100% transfer of single-crystal turbine blade and thermal barrier coating metallurgy.

Safran 110 kN Co-Development for AMCA Stealth Fighter

The most consequential technological breakthrough of the Horizon 2047 treaty is the joint development pact between France's Safran Aircraft Engines and India's Gas Turbine Research Establishment (GTRE). Unlike traditional licensed assembly, the agreement grants India unconditional sovereign access to single-crystal turbine blade metallurgy, ceramic matrix composites, and full authority digital engine control (FADEC) source code for the 5th-generation Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA Mk2).

Establishes sovereign high-temperature metallurgy engine independence and seals naval strike dominance across the Indian Ocean.
