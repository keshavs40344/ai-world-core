National Green Hydrogen Mission Crosses 5 Million Tonne Electrolyser Tender Benchmark

SECI concludes competitive bidding under SIGHT scheme, unlocking ₹1.2 lakh crore in private capital for green ammonia and steel plants.

NEW DELHI — In an unparalleled acceleration of India's statutory net-zero decarbonization roadmap, the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) has announced that total competitive tenders awarded under the Strategic Interventions for Green Hydrogen Transition (SIGHT) scheme have officially breached 5 million metric tonnes per annum (MMTPA) of production capacity.

SIGHT Scheme Allocations and Electrolyser Manufacturing

Conducted through the Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI), the latest auction round witnessed domestic and international energy conglomerates bidding aggressively for both green hydrogen production and indigenous electrolyser manufacturing incentives. Electrolyser manufacturing capacity bids totaling 3,000 MW per annum across Alkaline, Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM), and Solid Oxide (SOEC) technologies were awarded at record low incentive curves.

The Union Minister of State for New and Renewable Energy remarked: 'India is not entering the hydrogen economy as an importer of technology, but as a primary exporter. With renewable generation tariffs amongst the lowest globally at ₹2.40 per kWh, our green hydrogen production cost is on track to drop below $1.50 per kilogram before 2030, outcompeting fossil fuels.'

Decarbonizing Heavy Industry: Fertilizer, Steel, & Shipping

The awarded capacity is tied to binding domestic offtake agreements in the steel and fertilizer sectors. Fertilizer manufacturers will substitute gray ammonia with certified green ammonia, while major domestic steel producers in Odisha and Jharkhand are commissioning pilot green hydrogen direct reduced iron (DRI) blast furnaces to produce zero-carbon 'green steel'.

Key Targets of the National Green Hydrogen Mission

  • • Annual Production Target: 5 MMTPA Green Hydrogen capacity by 2030
  • • Dedicated Renewable Capacity Addition: Over 125 GW of solar and wind generation
  • • Cumulative Capital Investment: ₹8 Lakh Crore ($96 Billion) private and public expenditure
  • • Abatement of Greenhouse Gas Emissions: 50 Million Metric Tonnes of annual CO2 avoided

Export Hubs at Kandla, Paradip, and Tuticorin

To capture lucrative export markets in the European Union and Japan, designated coastal green hydrogen hubs are being developed at Kandla (Gujarat), Paradip (Odisha), and Tuticorin (Tamil Nadu). Deep-draft port berths with specialized cryogenic liquid hydrogen and green ammonia bunkering infrastructure will allow bulk carrier shipments commencing 2027.

Cuts fossil fuel imports by ₹1 lakh crore annually while establishing India as the undisputed green chemical manufacturing capital of the world.
