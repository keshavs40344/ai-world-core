National Green Hydrogen Mission Crosses 5 Million Tonne Electrolyser Tender Benchmark

Massive capital allocation and production-linked incentives position India as the lowest-cost green ammonia exporter to Europe and East Asia.

NEW DELHI — In a transformative leap toward industrial decarbonization and energy sovereignty, the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy has announced that India's National Green Hydrogen Mission has officially crossed 5 million metric tonnes per annum (MMTPA) of cumulative electrolyser and green ammonia capacity awarded under competitive bidding.

Core Investigative Findings & Key Facts

  • ▪National Green Hydrogen Mission crosses 5 MMTPA awarded production capacity benchmark.
  • ▪Record-low production cost of $2.60/kg achieved under competitive bidding.
  • ▪Replaces fossil gas in fertilizers, refineries, and pilot green steel plants.
  • ▪Export agreements finalized with Port of Rotterdam and Japanese industrial utilities.

Competitive Bid Outbids Global Benchmarks

The tender rounds, administered through the Solar Energy Corporation of India (SECI) under the Strategic Interventions for Green Hydrogen Transition (SIGHT) program, achieved a record-breaking production cost of $2.60 per kilogram of green hydrogen—the lowest levelized production cost recorded globally to date.

Key industrial conglomerates, including Indian Oil Corporation, Larsen & Toubro, Reliance Green Energy, and NTPC Green, were awarded production mandates alongside domestic electrolyser manufacturing subsidies. The facilities will leverage round-the-clock (RTC) renewable power generated from massive solar-wind hybrid complexes in Gujarat and Rajasthan.

Decarbonization of Heavy Industries

The green hydrogen produced will directly displace fossil natural gas in urea fertilizer manufacturing and imported grey hydrogen in petroleum refineries. Pilot installations for 100% green steel smelting using hydrogen direct reduced iron (H-DRI) processes are set to commence operations in Odisha and Karnataka by early 2027.

Mission Milestones at a Glance

  • • Cumulative Awarded Capacity: 5.12 Million Metric Tonnes Per Annum (MMTPA)
  • • Levelized Production Cost: $2.60 / kg (World's lowest competitive bid)
  • • Electrolyser Manufacturing Mandate: 3.2 GW annual domestic stack fabrication capacity
  • • Fossil Fuel Displacement: Projected reduction of ₹1.1 Lakh Crore in imported LNG and crude over 5 years

Export Corridors to Europe and Japan

India has simultaneously ratified green maritime shipping corridors with the Port of Rotterdam and Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI). The long-term offtake agreements will see sovereign Indian ports like Kandla and Paradip export green ammonia to decarbonize heavy commercial transport across European and Asian supply chains.

Strategic & Civic Impact Analysis

Insulates India from volatile global fossil fuel price shocks while creating a dominant domestic clean tech manufacturing export sector.
